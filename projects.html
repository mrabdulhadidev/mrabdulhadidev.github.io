What I've built

2025 — 2026

Elitra

A restaurant and home-kitchen marketplace needed one platform that could handle four different user types — customers, restaurants, home kitchens, and admins — without the system falling over under real traffic.

Built the full order lifecycle: cart, checkout, manual payment verification, live status tracking, and a disputes-and-refunds flow with a per-customer refund wallet. Real-time chat and order sync run through Supabase with sub-100ms broadcast latency. Also designed the full brand identity — logo, type system, color palette.

StackFlutter 3.10.6, Neon PostgreSQL, Supabase (Auth, Storage, Realtime), GitHub Actions
User rolesCustomer, Restaurant, Home Kitchen, Administrator
Scale47 screens, 19 database tables, load-tested to 10,000+ concurrent users
SecurityTLS 1.2+, automated backups, full audit logging, GDPR-compliant handling
github.com/teamchama23-sys/Elitra.pro
2025

Elice

A personal and business finance app that needed to work with zero internet connection — including the AI assistant.

Runs a quantized 1.5-billion-parameter language model (Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct) fully on-device via llama.cpp. Handles CSV and bank statement import, expense and investment tracking, loan/EMI tracking, savings goals, a business ledger, and simplified tax estimation — across seven local database tables with schema versioning.

StackFlutter (Dart), SQLite (sqflite), fllama (llama.cpp), Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (GGUF, Q4_K_M)
Runs onFully offline, on-device — no internet required for any feature
Data7 local SQLite tables with schema versioning across app updates
ModulesExpenses, investments, loans/EMI, savings goals, business ledger, tax estimation
github.com/teamchama23-sys/Elice
2024

MCF — Minhas Cattle Farm

A working cattle farm tracking registries, births, and health by hand needed one dashboard for the whole operation.

Built a live dashboard summarizing total cattle, calves born, and net profit/loss. An 8-category expense tracker feeds an automated profit-and-loss report, exportable as PDF and shareable straight to WhatsApp — plus daily reminders and multi-currency support for cross-border sales.

StackAndroid, SQLite, developed on Termux
TracksCattle registry, birth registry, health status, 8 expense categories
ReportingAutomated profit & loss report, PDF export, WhatsApp sharing
CurrenciesPKR, USD, AED, GBP, EUR
github.com/abdulhadige/MCF-cattle-farm
